# Intro Pitch — ML Engineering Manager Identity

About 75 seconds spoken. One identity, stated in the first sentence.

"Nice to meet you. I lead AI engineering teams that ship machine learning systems into production. At Moderna, my org builds LLM applications over manufacturing and operations data — retrieval pipelines, evaluation, and the guardrails these systems need to run inside a regulated company.

Before Moderna I spent twelve years at Amazon, most recently leading Alexa teams, where I worked on the multi-agent architecture behind the assistant. I started as a software development engineer, and my background is computer science, bachelor's and master's, so I stay close enough to the code to debug alongside my team.

I am moving back toward hands-on ML leadership by choice. The next stage at Moderna goes deep into biology. I want the next decade to be about core technology, at a company where the technology is the product, leading a team that owns models running in production."

## Notes for delivery

- The first sentence is the whole positioning. Everything after it is evidence.
- "Debug alongside my team" preempts the hands-on question before anyone asks it.
- The close names what you want (core technology, models in production) without listing three separate reasons. Save the "why this role" specifics for when they ask.
